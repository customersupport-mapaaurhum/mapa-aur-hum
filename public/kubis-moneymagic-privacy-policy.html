Kubi's MoneyMagic

Privacy Policy — How we look after your family's information, in plain language

Data Fiduciary: MapaAurHum Technologies OPC Pvt Ltd ("Company", "we", "us")
Effective date: 9 September 2026
Grievance / Data Protection contact: customer.support@mapaaurhum.com

Kubi's MoneyMagic helps kids learn about money through fun lessons, games, XP, and badges — and, when a parent switches it on, gentle practice managing their own pocket money. This policy explains what information we collect to make that work, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe, in plain language, because it should be genuinely readable by a parent, not just a lawyer.

Because Kubi's MoneyMagic is designed for children, this policy focuses on how we handle a child's information specifically. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") treats anyone under 18 as a "child" and gives their data extra protection — so wherever this policy says "child," that's what we mean, whatever age band your child is using inside the app.

1. Information we collect

1.1. About the parent or guardian

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Account credentials (e.g. password, or sign-in details if you use a login provider)

1.2. About the child

  • A nickname chosen by you or your child — not their legal name
  • An avatar (a cartoon icon) — not a real photo
  • An age band (for example, Junior 6–10 or Intermediate 10–14), used only to show age-appropriate lessons
  • Learning and game progress: which lessons/games are completed, quiz answers, XP earned, and badges unlocked
  • Pocket-money management data, if and when that feature is switched on for your family — for example, amounts logged, spending categories, and a savings goal

1.3. About how the app is used

  • Basic technical information needed to run the app (device type, app version, crash/error logs) — used to fix bugs, not to build a profile of your child
  • Aggregate, non-identifying statistics such as "most families finish onboarding within one day" — computed across many families, never as a per-child history

1.4. Consent records

  • A record of which version of these policies you were shown, which parent account accepted them, and the date and time you accepted — kept as proof of consent, not shared or used for any other purpose

2. Why we collect this information

  • To create and run your family's account, and let a parent and child use the app together.
  • To show your child lessons and games matched to their age band.
  • To track progress and award XP/badges, so learning feels rewarding.
  • To power the optional pocket-money management feature, when enabled.
  • To keep the app secure — for example, verifying you're the parent before showing parent-only settings.
  • To improve the app using aggregate statistics only, never an individual child's behaviour.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including keeping the consent records the DPDP Act requires.

3. How we protect it

  • All data is hosted on secure servers located in India, on AWS's Indian data-centre region.
  • We use industry-standard security practices (encryption in transit, access controls, regular patching). No system can guarantee absolute protection, but we take this seriously and review it regularly.
  • Access to family and child data inside our company is limited to people who need it to run or support the app.

4. How long we keep it

How long we keep different kinds of information depends on what it is and why we collect it. Here is the full breakdown:

4.1. Money Purse (transaction) data

  • We keep pocket-money management data — logged amounts, spending categories, and savings goals — for 30 days, then delete it automatically. We do not keep a yearly spending history for a child's account.

4.2. Learning progress, XP, and badges

  • We keep lesson/game completion records, quiz answers, XP, and badges for as long as your family's account is active, so your child doesn't lose their progress. This data is deleted when the account is deleted (see Section 9).

4.3. Account and profile data

  • Parent and child profile details (name, email, mobile number, nickname, avatar, age band) are kept for as long as your family's account is active.

4.4. Inactive accounts

  • If a family's account has not been used for 24 months, we will email the parent to check whether the account should stay open. If we do not hear back within 60 days of that notice, we will treat this as a withdrawal of consent and delete the account and its data, in line with Sections 8 and 9 of this Policy.

4.5. Technical logs

  • Device/app crash and error logs are kept for 90 days, purely to help us find and fix bugs, then deleted.

4.6. Consent records

  • Records of which policy version you accepted, and when, are kept for as long as your account is active, and for 3 years after your account is closed — so we can demonstrate, if ever asked by you or a regulator, that valid consent was given. After that period, consent records are deleted unless a longer legal retention applies (see Section 9.3).

4.7. Backups

  • Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days after deletion, purely as a safeguard against accidental data loss, before being permanently purged. It is not accessed or used for any other purpose during that time.

Wherever a specific retention period isn't listed above, we keep personal data only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected for (Section 2), or as required by law.

5. Who we share it with

  • Within the app, only as needed to run features you use — for example, a child's progress is visible to their own parent account, not to other families.
  • With service providers who help us run the app (such as our cloud hosting provider), under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only for that purpose.
  • With authorities, only if required by law.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share a child's data with advertisers.

6. Consent — how it works for a child's account

Because a child cannot legally give consent for their own data under the DPDP Act, we ask the parent or legal guardian to give verifiable consent before any child data is processed. In practice, this means:

  • During sign-up, you confirm you are the parent/legal guardian and accept these Terms and this Privacy Policy on your child's behalf, before any child profile is created.
  • We record which policy version you accepted and when, as described in Section 1.4.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your family's account — see Section 9.

7. Your rights as a parent or guardian

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access and review the information we hold about your family and your child.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (which will also lead to your child's account being closed, since we can no longer process their data without consent).
  • Request deletion of your family's account and data — see Section 9 below, which sets out the full process.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied with our response, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, write to customer.support@mapaaurhum.com. We may ask you to verify your identity first, to make sure we're speaking to the right parent/guardian and not exposing your child's data to someone else.

8. Deleting your account and data

Here is exactly what to expect if you ask us to delete your family's account and data:

8.1. How to ask

Email customer.support@mapaaurhum.com with your full name, the registered email address on the account, and your mobile number (if you gave us one). You can also start this from Settings → Delete account in the app.

8.2. What happens next

  • We'll confirm we received your request within 7 business days.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity before we proceed, to protect your family's data.
  • We will complete the deletion within 30 days of verifying your request, unless the law requires us to keep certain records longer (see below).
  • You'll get a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

8.3. What we may still keep, and why

A small amount of data may need to be kept a little longer, only where the law requires it, such as:

  • Tax, accounting, or audit records
  • Records needed to investigate fraud or security issues
  • Records needed to resolve an active dispute
  • Consent records — kept as proof that consent was properly given and later withdrawn (see Section 4.6)

Anything kept for these reasons is deleted as soon as it's no longer legally required, and is never used for any other purpose in the meantime.

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes — for example, if we launch a new feature that collects different information. We will let you know through the app or by email, and, where the change is material, ask you to re-confirm your consent. The date at the top of this document always reflects the version you're reading.
