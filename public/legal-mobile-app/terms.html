The parent or legal guardian who creates the account is the person we consider responsible for it. If more than one adult in a household uses the app (for example, both parents), please agree between yourselves who manages settings like the Parent PIN and account deletion.

Kubi's SeekhoPaisa helps children build healthy money habits through:

  • Bite-sized lessons and mini-games about saving, spending, budgeting, and how money works.
  • Optional pocket-money tracking ("Coin Purse"), logging allowance, simple spending categories, and a savings goal, once that feature is switched on for your account.
  • Rewards like XP points and badges for completing lessons.
