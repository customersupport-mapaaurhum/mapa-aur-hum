Request account & data deletion

Parents can ask us to permanently delete their Kubi's MoneyMagic family account and all the data linked to it, including their child's profile. This page explains what is deleted and how to send us your request.

This page does not delete anything by itself. Submitting a request means sending us an email. We review it, confirm that it comes from the account holder, and then carry out the deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. Email customer.support@mapaaurhum.com.
  2. Send it from the email address you registered with and include your full name, your registered email address and your mobile number (if you gave us one).
  3. State clearly that you want your family's account and data deleted.

Email my deletion request

If the button doesn't open your email app, copy this message into an email to customer.support@mapaaurhum.com:

Subject: Kubi's MoneyMagic – Account and data deletion request Hello, I am the parent/guardian account holder and I request permanent deletion of my Kubi's MoneyMagic family account and all associated data, including my child's profile. Full name: Registered email address: Mobile number (if provided): I understand that deletion is permanent and that any remaining paid access will not be refunded.

What happens next

  • We confirm we received your request within 7 business days.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity first, to make sure your family's data isn't exposed to someone else.
  • We complete the deletion within 30 days of verifying your request, unless the law requires us to keep certain records longer (see below).
  • You get a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

Deletion applies to the whole family account, the same as the Delete account option in the app. It permanently removes:

  • Your parent account details (name, email, mobile number, password) and your parent PIN.
  • Your child's profile: nickname, avatar and age band.
  • All learning progress: lessons, games, quiz answers, XP, diamonds, badges and challenges.
  • Coin Purse (pocket-money) data: amounts, spending categories and savings goals.
  • Notification device tokens, one-time codes and password-reset tokens.
  • Subscription, trial and purchase records held for your family.

What we may still keep, and for how long

Only where the law requires it, a small amount of data may be kept a little longer:

  • Tax, accounting or audit records.
  • Records needed to investigate fraud or security issues.
  • Records needed to resolve an active dispute.
  • Consent records (which policy version was accepted, by which parent account, and when). These are kept as proof that consent was properly given and later withdrawn, for 3 years after the account is closed.

Anything kept for these reasons is deleted as soon as it's no longer legally required and is never used for any other purpose in the meantime.

Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days after deletion as a safeguard against accidental loss, and is then permanently purged. It isn't accessed or used for anything else during that time.

Please note before you ask

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your family's data cannot be restored afterwards.

If you have paid access, it ends immediately and no refund is given for the remaining period.

Prefer to delete it yourself, right now?

You don't have to email us. In the app, go to Settings → Data & privacy → Delete account, enter your password to confirm, and your account and all its data are deleted straight away.

Questions or complaints

Write to our Grievance Officer at customer.support@mapaaurhum.com for anything about your data or this process. If you're not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

For full details, see the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions in the app under Settings → Data & privacy.
