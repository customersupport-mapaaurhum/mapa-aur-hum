Mapaaurhum MoneyMagic · Sticker Club — full review

Every screen, all 33, in blue and sun.

Everything merged into a single page, with the full prototype now redesigned to match and this round's feedback folded in. Use the Index (top right) to jump to any screen. Fonts are up across the board — screens now scroll where needed instead of shrinking text to fit, since a scroll is a much smaller price than small type for a kids' app.

What changed in this pass

  • Fonts enlarged everywhere — body text, labels and buttons are all a size up from the last round; screens scroll instead of compressing.
  • Home (child) simplified — dropped the Monthly Challenge banner and extra decoration; down to progress, today's lesson, and Explore.
  • Reports — removed the Family/You/child toggle; just Week/Month now.
  • Rewards split in two — Progress (streak, badges, weekly goal) and Play & Rewards (Challenge, Leaderboard, Friends) instead of one long page.
  • Badge Roadmap — bigger tiles, 3 per row instead of 4.
  • Leaderboard now ranks by points earned through learning, not ₹ saved, and gets its own "share your rank" action (moved off the Rewards page).
  • 11 new screens added — Friends, Learn, Intermediate syllabus, Settings (+ Nickname, Notifications, Privacy), Weekly recap, Upgrade, Refer a friend, and Feedback — the whole app is now covered, groups 8–10 below.
  • Mascot renamed Kuber → Kubi everywhere, and Meet Kubi's brand row is bigger and sits above the sky art, with a rupee badge added to the scene.
  • Onboarding screen removed as redundant; role select now leads straight into a new Enter parent PIN screen (tap the parent card's lock).
  • New expense — payment mode removed for the child version; Who's playing? intro line trimmed.
  • Reports — now weekly only (Month toggle dropped) and the by-day bar chart removed.
  • Play & Rewards — dropped the duplicate Friends row (it already lives on Home), added a standing card and made the remaining tiles bigger.
  • Leaderboard — added a Coin Purse savings % under every name.
  • Index added — the "Index" button (top right) opens a full list of all screens; tap any one to jump straight to it.
  • Fixed a layout bug — Friends & Learning, Settings, and Weekly recap were rendering sideways instead of as their own rows.
  • Full wordmark on every screen — tried per your ask: "Mapaaurhum MoneyMagic" now sits in the status bar row on every screen, not just the entry/exit ones. (First attempt floated it over the title and collided — moved it into the status bar itself so nothing overlaps.)
  • Feedback screen removed, "Not now" removed from Upgrade, Copy code + Email removed from Refer a friend, both timing dropdowns + test-notification button removed from Notifications, "Danger zone" renamed to "Delete account", Leaderboard rows decluttered and footer note shortened, Progress's goal card and Play & Rewards' Friends note removed, Learn's mascot icon fixed to the real Kubi photo, Home's "Play" tile renamed "Rewards", Sign in's redundant line removed, Weekly recap's extra insight row removed.
